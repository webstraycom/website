## Overview

The Settings section allows you to customize the application's appearance, manage security policies, and perform essential vault maintenance.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: Select between **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** preferences. Changes are applied instantly.

## Security

These settings define the identity verification policy for sensitive actions.

- **Require Verification**: The master toggle for security prompts. When disabled, the application will not ask for your Master Password for most sensitive actions.
- **Verification Timeout**: When verification is enabled, this sets the duration (in minutes) for which a successful prompt remains valid.
- **Change Master Password**: Allows you to rotate your primary credential. The app re-wraps your **Vault Key** at rest and refreshes the hardware-bound session; your saved entries stay encrypted with the same underlying key material.

## Data & Plugins

Direct access to data portability and application extensibility.

- **Import & Export**: Opens the interface for creating or restoring **JSON export** files. See [Import & Export](/docs/authenticator/user-guide/import-and-export) for details.
- **Plugins**: Manage third-party plugins, allowing you to enable or disable additional functionality. See [Plugins](/docs/authenticator/user-guide/plugins) for details.

## Maintenance

Tools for managing the health and lifecycle of your local database.

- **Compact Database**: Optimizes the local file by removing internal fragmentation. Recommended for maintaining performance.
- **Clear Database**: Permanently deletes absolutely all records, settings, and session tokens, returning the application to its initial state.

<Callout>
  Clearing the database is irreversible. Use this only if you intend to start over or are preparing
  to restore from a backup.
</Callout>

## Summary

The **Settings** section is the central hub for fine-tuning your experience. Use it to balance convenience and security, manage your plugins, and ensure your vault stays optimized through regular maintenance.
